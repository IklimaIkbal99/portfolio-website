Background

I studied architecture at Istanbul Technical University, then moved to Milan for a master's degree in Digital and Interaction Design at Politecnico di Milano. My architecture background shaped how I think about space, systems, and technical precision, and I carry that into every digital project I work on.

For my thesis I designed a game for neurodivergent and neurotypical children, combining inclusive design research, interaction design, and play theory. Game design is central to how I think and work. The constraints and rules of a game expose design problems that other methods often miss.

I love finding ways to make design engaging, not just functional. Bringing game thinking into UX, things like clear feedback loops, meaningful progression, and small rewards for curiosity, helps me create experiences people actually want to use.
