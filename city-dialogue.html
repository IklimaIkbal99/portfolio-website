CityDialogue

CityDialogue was developed during a workshop in collaboration with NetZeroCities, within the European Union context, responding to a brief for a digital platform that could strengthen knowledge exchange around climate-neutral cities.

Design Work

01 About CityDialogue

Our approach focused on turning a network into an accessible and structured digital experience. The platform brings together live discussion rooms, topic-based conversations, expert knowledge, shared resources, archived discussions, and user profiles, allowing participants to both contribute to and explore an evolving knowledge community.

The project explored how digital participation and community-driven knowledge sharing can support the wider transition toward climate-neutral cities.

The CityDialogue website is designed as a digital community hub where users can discover experts, join topic-based discussions, and exchange knowledge around climate-neutral cities. Through live discussion rooms, archived conversations, shared resources, messaging, and personalized profiles, the platform creates a structured space for collaboration and long-term knowledge sharing.
